The lights are back on at Hillview.

For years, 21 Buxton Road has been the spot neighbors stopped for a sandwich, a coffee or a lottery ticket. In 2026 a local family took it over, and Hillview Market & Deli is open again — with a fresh deli menu and the same neighborhood feel.

Come say hi. We’re glad you’re here, and we’re thankful for every one of you supporting local.
